# Guarantee portability rejection assay R1 — frozen procedure

2026-09-09. Assay only. No family admission, CLI feature, catalog migration or Scope change
is authorized by this protocol. Parent work: `wrk-guarantee-assay-r1`.
Experiment: `e-86e08561c32f`.

## Hypothesis and stopping rule

A bounded standard contract can express concrete properties across unrelated projects,
help a reader distinguish scope and falsifiers, and be suggested from attributed taxonomy
without confusing relevance with satisfaction. Each of these is a separate hypothesis.
The four selection topics are boundary validation, deterministic processing, projection
fidelity and persistence. Four is a queue, not a shipping quota.

If every frozen candidate has a representation failure, the first revision is rejected;
do not add implementation work or rescue it with post-hoc custom clauses. Unknown evidence
blocks admission but is not a demonstrated semantic failure. Repairing a candidate requires
a new revision/round with retained failures and additional fresh cases.

## Ordering and exposure

1. Independent curator selects new subjects from source responsibilities and failures,
   retaining all 17 known lab cases as a labeled stress set. The curator receives four
   responsibility topics, never candidate definitions or prior retrospective mapping scores.
2. Curator freezes `curation/corpus.json` and `curation/reference.json` by raw-byte SHA-256
   in `curation/freeze.json`, then reports only hashes/counts/readiness to the author.
   Missing population requirements are explicit; subjects are never padded to meet a gate.
3. Author drafts `candidates.json` only after curation freezes, without opening the corpus
   cases or reference answers. It records curation hashes and author exposure. Freeze its
   raw-byte digest in the decision journal before reviewer dispatch. No in-place revision
   after review starts. Author may then inspect all evidence for synthesis.
4. Two new context-isolated agents review the frozen corpus and candidate bytes. Neither
   receives curation reference answers, prior adoption mappings, author rationale outside
   the candidate document, or the other's results. Each writes to a separate directory and
   includes input digests and a disclosure of actual context exposure.
5. Reviewer output is frozen before either is shown the other output. The author joins
   their judgments to the curator's references, preserving disagreements and all unknowns.
6. Run a separate local-only versus candidate-assisted usefulness test only for revisions
   still eligible after representation review. New readers see one condition per case,
   with alternating assignments and no reference answers or review results. Do not recycle
   representation reviewers as uncontaminated usefulness readers.

This is procedural independence, not different-model or statistical independence. All
agents inherit the same model unless explicitly authorized otherwise; model correlation and
shared-filesystem exposure remain limitations. Read scopes and self-disclosed exclusions are
not an OS-enforced information barrier. No author-picked sources count as independent
curation, and no re-read by the author counts as an independent reviewer.

## Population and pass criteria

For each candidate: at least six curator-positive subjects across three projects and two
application domains, and at least three non-applicable near-neighbors across two projects.
Fresh means a new subject beyond the 17 known cases, not necessarily a newly acquired repo.
Report fresh and known counts separately. Every selected subject stays in the overall
matrix, even where the curator cannot establish relevance. No source execution is required
or authorized; a source-reading uncertainty remains uncertainty.

Representation admission requires every frozen positive represented and every frozen
negative excluded by both reviewers, with agreement on the essential scope and clause
meaning. A conforming and a violating observation must be concrete and separable. A project
violating a faithfully represented contract is not a representation failure. A definition
that excludes a curator-positive case, demands new clauses, weakens a falsifier, or needs an
undeclared parameter fails this revision. Reviewer disagreement or missing information is
unknown; the author cannot break a tie by majority with their own judgment.

Representability is not established by attaching a name to local prose. Scope parameters
may identify source addresses, domain members, formats, fixed constants, named observations
and an allowed mode. They may not contain an arbitrary predicate, injected natural-language
contract, new policy algorithm, or a post-hoc effective-input exemption. Reviewers distinguish
missing source evidence from a concrete semantic mismatch.

Usefulness admission requires at least two corrected answers across different projects,
no previously correct answer made incorrect, and no new component-wide safety inference.
Questions and reference answers are frozen by the curator before candidates. Record time
descriptively, never infer time from token counts. Unrun usefulness is not a passing score.
If representation already rejects a candidate, usefulness may remain unrun with that reason.

## Activation is a separate result

The deterministic rule maps independently recorded roles/facets to a candidate suggestion.
Its result is not a project obligation or a satisfaction verdict. If curator roles/facets
are unknown, the reference-taxonomy activation check is unknown, not silently guessed from
the curator's positive/negative label. Separately, reviewers may assess roles/facets directly
from the dossier; preserve the assessor and evidence before applying the same rule.

False activation or missed applicable cases blocks claiming dependable automatic suggestion
for that family. A failed activation mechanism does not by itself refute an otherwise useful
explicitly selected definition. Conversely a matching role cannot redeem a failed definition.
Unknowns must appear in both the classification and suggestion results. No changes to the
production classifier, catalog, assessments, or evidence records are part of this assay.

## Reviewer output contract

Each `review.json` includes reviewer identity, input SHA-256 digests, exposure disclosure,
and one row per corpus subject per topic. Required row fields:

- `caseId`, `topic` (`boundary`, `determinism`, `projection`, `persistence`).
- `applicability`: `applicable`, `not-applicable`, or `unknown`, with source-based reason.
- `representation`: `fit`, `mismatch`, `not-applicable`, or `unknown`.
- `parameters`: the proposed closed-schema instantiation; no extra keys or prose predicates.
- `clauseFindings`: each candidate clause's expressibility, independent of implementation pass.
- `conformingObservation`, `violatingObservation`, `reason`, `counterexample` (null if none).
- `taxonomy`: assessed roles/facets, unknown dimensions, and source evidence (or explicit unknown).
- `suggestion`: `candidate`, `not-selected`, or `unknown`, derived from that taxonomy only.
- `scopeSignature`: compact normalized meaning; reviewers' spelling differences are not proof
  of disagreement, but unresolved semantic differences remain unknown during synthesis.

There is no satisfaction field. Do not judge whether an entire repository is healthy.
Known stress cases with inadequate dossier evidence still receive explicit unknown rows.

## Evidence and authority limits

Raw source excerpts and digests establish what was read, not that tests ran. The known lab
dossiers have valid content digests but their historical source-commit equivalence was not
established by the prior readiness inventory. New dossier source pins are observations of
the current local checkout, labeled accordingly. Source, corpus and candidate changes after
freeze invalidate this round instead of being normalized away.

The final report separates population adequacy, representation, activation, usefulness and
procedural validity. Checks on file counts/hashes are mechanical; semantic judgments remain
attributed. A completed assay can honestly reject every candidate. No result here alters the
repository's previously failing full-verification report or creates a guarantee receipt.
